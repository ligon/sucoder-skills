#+TITLE: Drawer House Rules
#+DESCRIPTION: Local expectations for Org drawers.

* LOGBOOK
- Store state-change notes and clocks in `LOGBOOK` drawers (`org-log-into-drawer t`, persistent clocking).

* PROPERTIES
- Keep property drawers immediately below the heading (after planning lines). Use lowercase directives elsewhere.

* Notes Drawer
- Consider `:NOTE:` drawers for scratch content rather than leaving long inline comments.

Append new conventions as workflows evolve.
