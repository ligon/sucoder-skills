#+TITLE: Dates and Times House Rules
#+DESCRIPTION: Local defaults for scheduling and agenda behavior.

* Scheduling & Agenda
- Skip scheduled tasks in the global TODO list: `(setq org-agenda-skip-scheduled-if-done t)`.
- Enable diary integration for agenda views: `(setq org-agenda-include-diary t)`.

* Capture Templates
- Default capture to the system‐specific refile file: `~/Assistant/in.org`.
- Habits template uses `SCHEDULED: %t .+1d/3d` and sets `:STYLE:` and `:REPEAT_TO_STATE:` properties.

* Clocking
- Persist clocks between sessions: `(setq org-clock-persist t)` and `(org-clock-persistence-insinuate)`.

Append new scheduling or timing conventions as they evolve.
