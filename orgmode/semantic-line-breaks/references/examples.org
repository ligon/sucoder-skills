#+TITLE: Semantic Line Breaks: Worked Examples and Abbreviation Register
#+DESCRIPTION: Before/after conversions, plus the growing list of periods that do not end a sentence.

This file is written one sentence per line, so it doubles as a specimen.
Append to the register in section 2 whenever a new false terminator turns up in real prose.

* 1. Before and after

** 1.1 A plain paragraph

Before, wrapped at a fill column:

#+begin_example
  Households smooth consumption imperfectly, and the residual variance is
  informative about the market structure they face.  We estimate that
  residual on panel data from three countries, then ask whether the
  implied insurance coefficient is stable across them.
#+end_example

After:

#+begin_example
  Households smooth consumption imperfectly, and the residual variance is informative about the market structure they face.
  We estimate that residual on panel data from three countries, then ask whether the implied insurance coefficient is stable across them.
#+end_example

Note that the comma in the first sentence is not a break point.
Long lines are the expected outcome, not a defect to be wrapped away.

** 1.2 A sentence interrupted by display math

#+begin_example
  Let the planner maximize the weighted sum
  \begin{equation}
    \sum_{i} \lambda_i u_i(c_i),
  \end{equation}
  subject to the aggregate resource constraint.
  The weights \(\lambda_i\) are the objects we recover.
#+end_example

The sentence resumes immediately below =\end{equation}= with no blank line, so Org still reads one paragraph.
A blank line there would split the sentence into two paragraphs and put vertical space in the exported document.

** 1.3 A multi-sentence list item

#+begin_example
  - The instrument is rainfall at the village level.
    It is plausibly exogenous to household effort, though not to village-level investment.
  - Selection :: Attrition is nonrandom in wave three.
    We reweight using the inverse propensity of remaining in the panel.
#+end_example

Continuation sentences align under the item's text, not under the bullet.
For a description list, the tag and the first sentence share the =::= line.

** 1.4 A conversion that should not have happened

#+begin_example
  We follow the argument of Deaton (1992, Ch.
  6), who shows that the permanent-income benchmark fails here.
#+end_example

=Ch.= is not a sentence terminator, and the break has severed a citation.
The correct layout keeps the whole sentence on one line.

* 2. Abbreviation register

Periods that do not end a sentence.
Extend this list rather than rederiving it.

- Latin and reference :: =e.g.=, =i.e.=, =cf.=, =et al.=, =viz.=, =ibid.=, =op. cit.=, =vs.=, =approx.=, =resp.=
- Structural pointers :: =Fig.=, =Tab.=, =Sec.=, =App.=, =Ch.=, =p.=, =pp.=, =no.=, =vol.=, =eq.=, =Prop.=, =Thm.=, =Lem.=, =Cor.=, =Def.=, =Assum.=
- Titles and names :: =Dr.=, =Prof.=, =Mr.=, =Ms.=, =Mrs.=, =St.=, and single-letter initials such as =E. A. Ligon=
- Initialisms :: =U.S.=, =U.K.=, =U.N.=, =D.C.=, =Ph.D.=, =M.A.=
- Organizations and units :: =Inc.=, =Ltd.=, =Dept.=, =Univ.=, =kg.=, =approx.=
- Numerals :: any decimal (=0.5=, =1.96=), version strings (=Python 3.11=), and dotted section numbers (=2.3.1=)
- Punctuation :: an ellipsis (=...=), which terminates nothing on its own

Two cases resist any list.
A sentence ending in an abbreviation takes a single period and does end there, as in "the estimates are reported in levels, not logs, etc."
A capitalized word following an abbreviation is ambiguous, as in "see Sec. Three below"; read the sentence rather than the pattern.

* 3. Checking a file

Lines holding more than one sentence are worth a look, though every hit needs a human read:

#+begin_src sh
  grep -n '[a-z][.!?] \+[A-Z]' paper.org
#+end_src

Breaks that landed after a known abbreviation are the more damaging error, and are easier to find:

#+begin_src sh
  grep -nE '\b(e\.g|i\.e|cf|et al|vs|Fig|Tab|Sec|Ch|p|pp|no|eq|Prop|Thm|Dr|Prof)\.$' paper.org
#+end_src

Neither command is a linter, and neither should be run over a file wholesale without reading what it reports.
Remember that untouched paragraphs stay untouched.
